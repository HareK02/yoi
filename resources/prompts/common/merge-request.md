## MR records

MR bookkeeping is separate from selecting and adopting changes. Use only the five model-facing MR operations: OpenMergeRequest, ShowMergeRequest, SetMergeRequestWorker, CommentMergeRequest and ReviewMergeRequest. At start/resume read the saved MR phase, worker_assignment, source, review and publications under current authority. The MR's current Worker is separate from the Ticket implementation assignment, and succession alone does not invalidate approval. In Integration the Coder may merge latest target into that MR source, resolve conflicts and validate within the chosen scope; changed source/requirements need fresh review, not a replacement MR or an aggregate of other MRs. Record conflict, decision request and resolution in MR comments. Subject chooses scope/target/order without a saved order ledger or stamp. Do not mutate source or resend while publication is in flight or unknown; yield without polling, then reread on notification/input. Flow instructions guide work but neither grant authority nor replace saved MR phases. Existing pinned Session definitions do not change merely because the catalog changed. Backend owns automatic publication and evidence-bound completion after independent approval, subject to current permissions and target conditions; no model completion call or additional Subject stamp is required. This MR approval requirement is not a general Git adoption gate. Partial or adapted adoption is not full MR integration.

`ShowMergeRequest` defaults to a compact `summary` computed from complete evidence, not the returned history page. For review bodies/findings or prior events, request `view: "detail"`, follow `event_page.next_after`, or use a returned event sequence with `after: sequence - 1, limit: 1`. `ShowMergeRequest.summary.readiness` is an observation, not permission to integrate; unknown source observations remain unknown.

When using MRs, address each by `merge_request_id`. Reuse the repository's open MR and its `selector_from`/`selector_to`; publish source updates with a normal non-force push. Before formal review, verify the published source matches committed HEAD. Keep review/fix evidence on the MR, not repeated Ticket comments.

Record validation evidence once on the MR or an accessible session entry, then cite it: source/tree or dirty snapshot identity, command and scope, relevant features/toolchain/environment, exit or timeout and coverage reached, log reference, and unresolved issues. Reuse evidence for unchanged code under equivalent conditions. For a new candidate, assess dependencies, generated code, lockfiles, features, harness/shared initialization, migrations, and environment—not just non-overlapping files—and rerun affected or uncertain checks. Distinguish reused evidence from tests actually executed on the new source. Classify failures as regression, reproduced on the baseline, environment-dependent, or unresolved; do not call an unclassified failure or a timeout success. Broad tests are appropriate when the impact warrants them, not automatically after every local fix.

Formal approval binds the source, Ticket `content_digest`, and linked MR/result snapshot. Changed source or requirements need fresh attestation; target-only movement preserves approval. Read `ShowMergeRequest.summary.readiness` for current eligibility and the saved publication evidence for outcomes; a read does not trigger or retry publication. Ticket close/work release does not require reopen/reassignment for MR processing.
